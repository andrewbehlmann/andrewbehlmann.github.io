Freditor Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Freditor is a Markdown editor that runs entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or usage data.

Your documents

Files you open, edit and save stay on your Mac, in the locations you choose. Freditor never uploads them anywhere.

Settings

Your preferences (font, size, appearance and autosave) are stored locally on your Mac using the standard macOS preferences system. They are not sent to us or anyone else.

No analytics, tracking or accounts

Freditor contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and has no user accounts. The app does not connect to the internet. When you click a link inside a document, it opens in your default web browser, which is governed by that browser's own privacy policy.

Purchases

If you buy Freditor from the Mac App Store, the purchase is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Because Freditor collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
